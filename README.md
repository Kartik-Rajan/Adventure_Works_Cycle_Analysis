# Adventure Works Cycle: Sales Data Analysis

End-to-end sales analysis of the Adventure Works cycle company, built with **MySQL, MS Excel, Power BI and Tableau**. The project turns raw sales data into KPIs and dashboards that show revenue, cost, profit, seasonality, regional performance and top products.

## 🎯 Business Questions
- How much revenue, cost and profit does the business generate, and how has it changed year over year?
- Which months and quarters drive the most sales?
- Which countries and regions are the biggest markets?
- Which products and customers contribute the most revenue?

## 🛠 Tools Used
| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **MySQL** | Querying, joining and aggregating the sales data |
| **MS Excel** | KPI calculations and Excel dashboard |
| **Power BI** | Interactive dashboard |
| **Tableau** | Interactive dashboard |

## 📈 Key Results
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | **$29.36M** |
| Total Production Cost | **$17.28M** |
| Total Profit | **$12.08M** |
| Profit Margin | **41.1%** |
| Unique Customers | **18,484** |
| Total Orders / Line Items | **27,659 / 60,398** |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Peak year:** annual sales peaked in **2013 at $16.35M**.
- **Seasonality:** **Q4 is the strongest quarter** at $9.11M (31.02% of revenue). December is the best month ($3.21M), with a mid-year rise in June ($2.94M).
- **Geography:** **Australia** leads with $9.06M (30.86%), followed by the **Southwest US** region at $5.72M (19.48%).
- **Products:** revenue is driven mainly by premium bikes, led by the **Mountain-200** (Black and Silver) and **Road-150 Red** models.

## 📊 KPIs Tracked
- **Financial & operational:** total sales, production cost, profit and margin, orders and line items, year-over-year trend and Q4 seasonality
- **Demographic & regional:** unique customers, sales by country and region, top 10 customers by revenue, top product models, sales vs. production cost

## 💡 Recommendations
1. **Plan for peaks:** adjust inventory, staffing and marketing for the mid-year surge and the Q4 / December peak.
2. **Replicate top markets:** apply what works in Australia and the Southwest US to weaker regions.
3. **Focus on high-margin models:** prioritize manufacturing and promotion for the Mountain-200 and Road-150 series.
4. **Retain top customers:** build VIP and loyalty programs for the highest-value buyers.
5. **Grow order value:** use cross-selling to increase average order size.
6. **Protect margin:** track sales against production cost so the margin stays stable as volume grows.
